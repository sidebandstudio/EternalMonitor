EternalMonitor - Quick Start
============================
EternalMonitor turns your iPad into a screen for this PC. You can mirror the
PC's screen, or add the iPad as a second screen next to your monitor. Touch,
Apple Pencil and a keyboard on the iPad control the PC.

Full guide with every screen explained:
https://github.com/sidebandstudio/EternalMonitor/blob/main/SETUP.md
Known issues and what to do about them:
https://eternalmonitor.dev/known-issues.html

What you need
-------------
- A 64-bit PC with Windows 10 version 1809 or later, or Windows 11.
- An NVIDIA, AMD or Intel graphics card. Without one that can encode video,
  the picture uses the processor and may lag.
- An iPad with iPadOS 17 or later.
- For Wi-Fi: the PC and iPad on the same home or office network. Guest
  networks usually block devices from seeing each other.
- For USB: a cable that carries data, and Apple's free Apple Devices app
  from the Microsoft Store, open on the PC while you stream. Apple Devices
  needs Windows 10 version 22H2 (build 19045) or Windows 11. On older
  Windows 10, use Wi-Fi.

Both apps must come from the same version of EternalMonitor. The download
page always lists the matching pair:
https://eternalmonitor.dev/download.html

1. Install the iPad app
-----------------------
On the iPad, install TestFlight from the App Store. Then open this invite on
the iPad and tap Accept and Install:
https://testflight.apple.com/join/ppc9QfXc

2. Connect over Wi-Fi
---------------------
1. Keep this EternalMonitor window open. It says "Ready for your iPad".
   You can minimize it. Closing it stops streaming.
2. Open EternalMonitor on the iPad. The first time, it asks to find devices
   on your local network. Tap Allow.
3. Tap your PC under "Found on this network". Or tap "Scan QR code" and
   point the iPad at the code from "QR code" on the PC. Or type the
   Address shown in this window and tap "Connect to PC".
4. The first time, the iPad shows "Pair with your PC". Type the six-digit
   Pairing code from this window and tap "Pair iPad". You only do this once.
   Scanning the QR code skips it.

If Windows asks about EternalMonitor in a Windows Security Alert, tick
Private networks and click Allow access.

3. Connect with a USB cable (optional)
--------------------------------------
A cable gives a steadier picture and charges the iPad. It needs Windows 10
version 22H2 (build 19045) or Windows 11, because Apple Devices does. To see
your version, open Windows Settings, System, About.
1. Install Apple Devices from the Microsoft Store, open it and leave it
   open. It can stay minimized.
2. Plug in the iPad and unlock it. The first time, tap Trust on the iPad.
3. Open EternalMonitor on the iPad. It connects over the cable by itself.
   No pairing code is needed.

If this window says "Your iPad is plugged in, but Apple Devices is not
running", click "Open Apple Devices". If it says Apple Devices is not
installed, click "Get Apple Devices".

4. Mirror or second screen
--------------------------
At first the iPad mirrors your main screen. At the top of the Stream page,
next to "Use the iPad as", click "Second screen" to use the iPad as an extra
screen. The iPad reconnects by itself. Drag a window past the edge of your
main screen to move it onto the iPad. "Arrange screens" opens Windows Display
settings so you can choose which side the iPad is on. To mirror again, click
"Mirror my screen". The choice is always made on the PC.

5. Using it
-----------
- Tap to click. Drag to move something. Two fingers scroll. Touch and hold
  to right-click.
- Tap with three fingers to show the controls: Keyboard, settings and
  Disconnect.
- Sound plays on the iPad when "Stream PC audio" is on in this window's
  Settings and "Play PC audio" is on in the iPad app's settings.
- To only watch, turn off "Control PC" in the iPad app's settings.
- To draw, turn on "Drawing mode" in the iPad app's settings, then tap
  "Reconnect now to apply". In Clip Studio Paint, choose Preferences, then
  Tablet, then Tablet PC. Apple Pencil (USB-C) has no pressure sensor.

6. Updates
----------
When a new version is out, this window says "EternalMonitor ... is
available". Click "Update now" and click Yes when Windows asks. If you are
streaming, the update waits until you stop. On the iPad, tap "Open
TestFlight", then Update.

If something goes wrong
-----------------------
Messages are quoted as the apps show them. The apps use curly apostrophes
in a few places, so search for a few words rather than the whole line.

What the iPad says, and what to do:
- "Your PC didn't answer": check that this window says "Ready for your
  iPad", the PC is awake, and both are on the same Wi-Fi, not a guest
  network. If so, a firewall is probably blocking EternalMonitor: run
  EternalMonitor-Setup.exe again, or use a USB cable.
- "Your PC answered, but no picture arrived": follow any message in this
  window, click Restart, then tap Try again on the iPad.
- "Lost the connection to your PC": wake the PC, check EternalMonitor is
  open, then tap Try again.
- "That looks like the pairing code, not the PC's address": type the
  Address from this window, not the Pairing code.
- "That code didn't match. Check the PC and try again.": type the six
  digits shown in this window now.
- "EternalMonitor isn't allowed on your local network": tap Open Settings
  and turn on Local Network.
- "The connection over the cable stopped": keep EternalMonitor and Apple
  Devices open, unlock the iPad, then unplug the cable and plug it in again.
- "This iPad app is older than EternalMonitor on your PC, so they can't
  connect. Update the iPad app: open TestFlight and tap Update.": tap Open
  TestFlight, then Update.
- "EternalMonitor on your PC is older than this iPad app, so they can't
  connect. Update EternalMonitor on your PC: click Update now on the PC, or
  download it from eternalmonitor.dev.": in this window, click "Update now".
  If the button says "Check for updates" instead, click it, then click
  "Update now" when it appears. If no update is found, click "Download from
  the website".

What this window says, and what to do:
- "The second screen couldn't start, so your main screen is mirrored":
  the line below says why.
  If it says Windows' display driver stopped responding, restart the PC.
  That message has no button.
  If it says the second screen isn't installed, or part of the install is
  missing or blocked, click "Download installer", run it, then click
  "Try again".
  If it says Windows didn't add the second screen in time, click
  "Try again". If that keeps happening, run EternalMonitor-Setup.exe again.
- "Video is using the processor instead of the graphics card": click
  "Use recommended settings" if shown. Otherwise update your graphics driver
  from NVIDIA, AMD or Intel, then click Restart.
- "Your PC is showing the lock screen or a permission prompt": unlock the
  PC or answer the prompt. The picture comes back by itself.
- "Update the iPad app to connect": on the iPad, open TestFlight and tap
  Update.

Getting help
------------
In this window, open Performance and click "Copy logs". In the iPad app's
settings, open Support, then Diagnostics, then "Export diagnostics". Send both
with what you tried to hello@sideband.studio, or use the form on
https://eternalmonitor.dev/download.html#feedback.

The stream is not encrypted. Pairing keeps other people's devices out, but
use EternalMonitor only on a network you trust, such as your home Wi-Fi.
Keep the QR code private: anyone who scans it can connect.
