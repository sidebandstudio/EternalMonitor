# Shot list

A short recording on real devices, about 30 to 45 seconds in all, made of
three shots. Film the desk from above or at a slight angle so the PC monitor
and the iPad are both in frame. Use a plain wallpaper, hide desktop icons and
notifications, and keep names, email and chat windows off both screens.

Until the recording exists, the site films in `docs/assets/demos/` stand in.
Each has a wide version (`name.mp4`, `name.webm`) and a phone-shaped one
(`name-m.mp4`). They are animations built around the apps' real interface, with
sample numbers, so they are good for a post but not a substitute for a real
recording, least of all in the art post.

## Shot 1. A window dragged onto the iPad

- iPad beside the monitor, connected, the PC app set to **Second screen**.
- Grab a window on the monitor and drag it across the edge onto the iPad. Let it
  settle and hold for two seconds.
- Optional second take. Scroll a long page on the iPad with two fingers, then
  tap a link.
- Stand-in now: `docs/assets/demos/second-screen.mp4`. The pointer picks Second
  screen in the Windows app, then carries a document off the monitor onto the
  iPad.

## Shot 2. Apple Pencil drawing in an art app

- Clip Studio Paint full screen on the iPad's display, set to **Tablet PC**
  (Preferences > Tablet). **Drawing mode** on in the iPad settings, connected
  over USB.
- Close-up on the iPad. Draw a few strokes that go from light to heavy, so the
  line visibly thickens. Then lay the Pencil over and shade with a brush that
  has tilt turned on.
- Rest your palm on the glass during a stroke. The stroke carries on, which
  shows palm rejection without a word of explanation.
- End on a wide shot with the PC monitor showing the same canvas.
- Stand-in now: `docs/assets/demos/pencil.mp4` (pressure strokes, then tilt
  shading).

## Shot 3. Plugging in USB takes over

- Start on Wi-Fi with the stream running and something moving on screen (a
  video or a scrolling page). Apple Devices open on the PC.
- Plug the cable into the iPad on camera. Hold on the iPad while the stream
  switches over and says **USB**.
- Optional. Unplug it and show the stream carry on over Wi-Fi.
- Stand-in now: none of the site films shows this. The closest is the "Three
  steps" clip on the home page, `docs/assets/connect-clip.mp4`, which shows
  connecting.
