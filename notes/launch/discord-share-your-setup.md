# Discord post: share your setup

For the Sideband Discord (https://discord.gg/Xc7NykBZ6z), in the main or
showcase channel. Pin it for a week.

---

**Show us your EternalMonitor setup**

How do you use your iPad with your PC? A second screen for chat or docs, a
mirror on the couch, a pen display for Clip Studio Paint? Post a photo of your
desk or something you drew with Apple Pencil, and tell us:

- your PC's graphics card (NVIDIA, AMD or Intel) and your iPad model
- Wi-Fi or USB
- what you use it for

We'd love to feature a few setups and drawings on eternalmonitor.dev and in
release posts. We'll always ask you first, by reply or DM, and credit you the
way you want. Nothing gets shared without a yes.

Tried it on hardware we haven't heard about yet? Fill in the compatibility
report so others with the same PC and iPad know what to expect:
https://github.com/sidebandstudio/EternalMonitor/issues/new?template=compatibility.yml

And if you like the app, a star on GitHub helps more people find it:
https://github.com/sidebandstudio/EternalMonitor
