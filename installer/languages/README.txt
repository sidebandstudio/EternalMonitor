Inno Setup messages for the two app languages Inno Setup has no official
translation for, copied from jrsoftware/issrc at tag is-6_7_3
(Files/Languages/Unofficial). The other nine languages use the official
files in the compiler's Languages folder.

  ChineseSimplified.isl  maintained by Zhenghan Yang
                         (github.com/kira-96/Inno-Setup-Chinese-Simplified-Translation),
                         unchanged
  Hindi.islu             translated by Him Prasad Gautam; written for Inno Setup
                         5.5.3, so messages added since then show in English.
                         Corrected for EternalMonitor: the language dialog, the
                         pages and buttons its installer and uninstaller show,
                         their status lines and a few errors were rewritten
                         against Inno Setup 6.7.3's Default.isl (spelling,
                         wording, "PC" for computer as in the app's glossary),
                         the language is named हिन्दी as in the app, and the
                         translator credit left the bottom of every page
                         (BeveledLabel, empty as in Default.isl). Pages the
                         installer never shows keep the original text.

Both are distributed with Inno Setup under its licence:
https://github.com/jrsoftware/issrc/blob/is-6_7_3/license.txt
