Updates

What's new in each release, and what it fixes.

0.3.2October 23, 2026

Apple Pencil lands under the tip, and the iPad can fill its screen.

  • Apple Pencil lands right under the tip, even with another monitor to the left of or above your main one.
  • New Fill option: the PC picture can cover the whole iPad screen. Fit still shows all of it.
  • Clearer messages when something goes wrong, each with a button to fix it, like Try again.
  • Send feedback from either app. It writes the email for you, and nothing is sent until you press Send.
  • A popup tells you when a new version is out. On Windows, Update now installs it for you.
  • No more black console window next to the app on Windows.
  • The PC app opens on graphics drivers that made 0.3.1 crash at startup.
  • Steadier USB and Wi-Fi: a USB connection stays up when the stream restarts, and the picture comes back by itself after the lock screen.

How to update

Windows
Download Setup from the download page and run it. From 0.3.2 on, Update now in the app does this for you.
iPad
Open TestFlight and tap Update. The iPad update rolls out through TestFlight.

0.3.1October 18, 2026

Better on Intel and AMD graphics.

  • PCs with Intel graphics encode the picture in hardware, and try the other options before falling back to the processor.
  • On AMD graphics, the picture recovers faster after a lost frame.
  • Less dropped sound while a lot of video is moving.
