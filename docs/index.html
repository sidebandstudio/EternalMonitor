    Before you install

    Is it really free?

    Yes. Free, open source under MIT, with no account.

    Does it work with a Mac?

    Not yet. Mac support is coming soon, for iPad and iPhone Duo; Apple's Sidecar doesn't work with an iPhone Duo. Until then, use Sidecar with an iPad.

    USB or Wi-Fi?

    Wi-Fi to start. A cable gives a steadier picture and charges the iPad; it needs Apple's free Apple Devices app open on the PC (Windows 10 22H2 or 11).

    Is the stream encrypted?

    Not yet. Pairing keeps other devices out, so use a network you trust. Over a cable, the stream stays on the cable.

    Which iPads and Pencils?

    An iPad with iPadOS 17 or later. Apple Pencil (USB-C) has no pressure sensor, so its strokes keep one width.

    Does it send anything to you?

    Yes, diagnostics while the apps run: device details, errors and stream stats, including network addresses. Never your screen, audio or pairing codes. From version 0.3.4, your PC's name, your Windows user name and the names you gave your iPad and audio devices are removed. Kept up to 30 days, sometimes less, and up to 7 more days in backups. Turn it off in Settings. Details.
