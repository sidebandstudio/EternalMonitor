Privacy

What the EternalMonitor apps send, why, and how to turn it off.

What the apps send

The Windows app and the iPad app send diagnostics to EternalMonitor support while they run:

  • Device details, such as app version, iPad model, Windows version and graphics hardware.
  • Errors and other lines from the apps' logs.
  • Stream statistics, such as frame rate and dropped frames.
  • PC names and local network addresses, which appear in these logs.
  • A random ID the app creates when it is installed. It is your Support ID, and it lets us find your diagnostics.

Why

To run the app, find out why a connection or the picture fails, and make the app better.

What is never sent

  • What's on your screen.
  • Audio.
  • Pairing codes and tokens. They are removed before anything is saved or sent.

Where it is stored

Uploads use encrypted HTTPS. They pass through Vercel and are stored in a Supabase database in the US East region.

How long

Diagnostics are kept for up to 30 days and may be removed sooner when storage limits are reached. Deleted or expired diagnostics can remain in daily backups for up to 7 more days.

Turn sharing off

Windows
Open Settings. Under Support diagnostics, turn off Automatically share diagnostics.
iPad
Open Settings, then Support, then Diagnostics, and turn off Automatically share diagnostics.

Turning it off clears anything waiting to upload. Diagnostics already sent are deleted on the schedule above. You can still export or copy a report yourself, and it only reaches us if you send it.

Ask us to delete your diagnostics

Email hello@sideband.studio with the Support ID shown in the app's diagnostics settings. We'll delete everything stored under that ID. Copies in backups are gone within 7 days.
