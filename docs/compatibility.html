    On a Mac?Mac support is coming soon, for iPad and iPhone Duo. Until then, EternalMonitor streams from Windows PCs, and Apple's Sidecar works with an iPad.

    Compatibility

    The PCs and iPads EternalMonitor runs on, and the results measured on each, with the conditions and the record behind every number.

    What it needs

    • A 64-bit PC with Windows 10 version 1809 or later, or Windows 11.
    • NVIDIA, AMD or Intel graphics. The graphics card encodes the video: NVENC, AMF or Quick Sync, in H.264 or HEVC.
    • An iPad with iPadOS 17 or later.
    • For Wi-Fi, the PC and the iPad on the same network. Guest networks usually keep devices apart.
    • For USB, a cable that carries data and Apple's free Apple Devices app, open on the PC. Apple Devices needs Windows 10 version 22H2 (build 19045) or Windows 11.
    • For pressure, Apple Pencil (1st or 2nd generation) or Apple Pencil Pro.

    Source: the setup guide.

    Tested setups

    Measured on September 23 and 24, 2026, on the v0.3.0 release candidate. The hardware verification record keeps every run with its evidence.

    The test PC and iPad

    PC
    Windows 11, AMD Ryzen 7 7800X3D with Radeon graphics, NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5080 on driver 591.86, a 3440 × 1440 main display.
    iPad
    iPad Pro 12.9-inch (M2), iPadOS 26.2.
    Connection
    The PC's home Wi-Fi network, and a USB-C to USB-C cable in the PC's rear USB-C port.

    Second screen over USB, 30 minutes

    Result
    Passed.
    Measured
    58.42 frames per second on average. 105,240 frames decoded, none dropped. Every one of the 60 half-minute intervals stayed at 55 fps or more.
    Conditions
    1,800 seconds as a second screen at the iPad's 2732 × 2048, on a build from just before Apple Pencil drawing was added.
